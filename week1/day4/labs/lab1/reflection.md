# Reflection
In our project, I saw how scrum is very important for devops because it keeps work small, visible. Sprint planning and the backlog help us deliver in small increments, which fits perfectly with continuous integration and continuous delivery. Daily standups reflects blockers early and issues don't block flow of work in long game, also it made blockers visible easliy and we can detect and solve them as soon as possible. Sprint reviews and retrospectives create fast feedback envoirement with team, team leads and stakeholders that assures everyone report anyting anytime and get feedbacks as soon as possible and bring up better product
